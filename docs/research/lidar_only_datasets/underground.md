# Underground / tunnel / mine / cave LiDAR datasets with public ground truth (for LiDAR-only degeneracy detection)

Research date: Oct 2026. About 22 tool calls. One page could not be read: Springer page for SubSurfaceGeoRobo (proxy HTTP 429 rate limit), so that dataset is listed as unverified.

## Key Question 1: Which datasets have LiDAR + IMU + a public GT trajectory, and which also have a public GT map?

### Takeaway
Confirmed underground or tunnel datasets that have LiDAR, IMU and a downloadable per-sequence GT trajectory: ENWIDE (tunnel), GEODE (metro, mining-method and urban tunnels), SubT-MRS (caves and long corridor; GT built from a FARO survey map), CERBERUS SubT Final (Mega Cavern), Hilti 2022/2023 (underground and basement sequences, sparse 3DoF GT only), M2TD (subsea and traffic tunnels, fused GT) and CAVERS (karst cave, MoCap). Public GT point-cloud maps are confirmed only for SubT-MRS (maps for the SuperLoc cave sequences), GEODE ("selected indoor scenarios" only) and the DARPA SubT circuit ground-truth scans (survey maps, but without per-robot GT trajectories).

### Candidates checked and dropped or not usable
- **Lidar Variability (arXiv 2507.04321)**: real paper (Felix et al., July 2025). It covers an indoor factory-like facility and outdoor roads only, **with no underground data**. GT is MoCap and RTK. At publication the data "will appear online soon". License CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Not relevant. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.04321v1))
- **GrandTour (arXiv 2602.18164)**: real paper (Frey, Tuna, Fu, Patterson, Xu, Fallon, Cadena, Hutter, Feb 2026). It has Hesai XT-32, Livox Mid-360 and VLP-16, 7 IMUs, Leica MS60 and RTK GT, and point-cloud maps ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18164v1), [site](https://grand-tour.leggedrobotics.com/)). **No underground, tunnel or mine missions were confirmed.** The README lists outdoor missions such as Grindelwald canyon and Pilatus hike ([GitHub](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/grand_tour_dataset)). The paper mentions "demolished buildings". Only the first 100k characters of the arXiv HTML were read, so a tunnel mission is not ruled out.
- **X-ICP Seemühle mine / Tuna et al. field analysis**: X-ICP (arXiv 2211.16335) and "Informed, Constrained, Aligned" (arXiv 2408.11809) use ETH field data: the Ulmberg bicycle tunnel (one-directional degeneracy), ANYmal forest and the HEAP excavator site. The authors say the **implementations** are public ([project page](https://sites.google.com/leggedrobotics.com/perfectlyconstrained)). **No public release of the Seemühle or Ulmberg data was found. Unverified.**
- **CTU-CRAS, CSIRO Wildcat, NeBula/JPL, Explorer SubT team datasets**: not verified in this pass. The official SubT resources list includes only DARPA GT scans, the ARL reference data, ColoRadar (MARBLE; mines, "Partial" GT) and SubT-MRS ([subt_resources](https://github.com/subtchallenge/subt_resources/blob/main/3-Datasets.md)). **Unverified.**
- **SubSurfaceGeoRobo** (Li et al. 2025, PFG / Springer; data on PANGAEA, [doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.975532](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.975532)): an underground SLAM geomonitoring dataset. The details page could not be read (HTTP 429). **Sensors and GT are unverified.**
- **ColoRadar**: 52 sequences in mines, buildings and an urban creek, 3D lidar and IMU, GT listed as "Partial" ([subt_resources](https://github.com/subtchallenge/subt_resources/blob/main/3-Datasets.md)). The LiDAR model and GT type were not verified.

### Inferences
- For geometry-based oracle labels (eigen-analysis of the Hessian against a GT map), the strongest options are **SubT-MRS / SuperLoc** (FARO survey map plus 6DoF GT plus VLP-16) and **GEODE** (indoor scenarios with released maps; RTC360 TLS confirmed for Stairs; whether the metro-tunnel maps are released is unclear).
- ENWIDE and the GEODE metro tunnels give position-only GT from a total station. Translational degeneracy can be judged from the drift along the tunnel axis, but rotational accuracy cannot be scored.
- M2TD GT comes from fusion with UWB, so it is not independent of LiDAR-IMU estimation. Use it with caution as an "oracle".

### Gaps
- Licenses for ENWIDE, GEODE, SubT-MRS and CERBERUS were not stated on the pages read.
- IMU rates for GEODE (Xsens MTi-30) and SubT-MRS (Epson M-G365) were not captured.
- Whether the GEODE metro-tunnel GT maps are released was not confirmed.
- Dataset sizes for ENWIDE, GEODE and SubT-MRS were not captured.

## Key Question 2: Which are 16-beam (closest to an A-LOAM VLP-16 setup)?

### Takeaway
Confirmed 16-beam underground data: **SubT-MRS** (Velodyne Puck 16; caves, corridors, tunnels), **CERBERUS SubT Final** (VLP-16 Puck LITE on ANYmal; Mega Cavern), **GEODE device α (VLP-16)** in the mining-method tunnels and urban tunnels (the shield metro tunnels were recorded only with OS1-64 and Avia), and **CAVERS** (VLP-16; cave rooms with MoCap).

### Cited Findings
- SubT-MRS LiDAR is a Velodyne Puck (16 beams) — [arXiv 2307.07607](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.07607v5)
- CERBERUS ANYmal uses a Velodyne VLP16 Puck LITE — [GitHub](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/cerberus_darpa_subt_datasets)
- GEODE α = VLP-16. The tunneling-method tunnels use α/β/γ; the urban tunnels use α only; the shield tunnels use γ (1–6) and β (7–10) — [GEODE homepage](https://thisparticle.github.io/geode/)
- CAVERS uses a VLP-16 — [arXiv 2604.15052](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15052v2)
- ENWIDE is an OS0-128 (128-beam) — [ENWIDE](https://projects.asl.ethz.ch/datasets/enwide/). Hilti uses PandarXT-32 — [Hilti 2022](https://hilti-challenge.com/dataset-2022)

### Inferences
- The best 16-beam tunnel and axial-degeneracy match with independent GT is the GEODE α tunneling-method tunnel (UGV, 150–206 m, total-station GT) and the GEODE α urban tunnels (vehicle, km-scale; outdoor-style GT, since the homepage lists GNSS/INS-RTK for outdoor data and the exact method for tunnels was not confirmed).
- ENWIDE OS0-128 data could be downsampled to 16 rings to approximate a VLP-16, but the vertical FoV differs (OS0 is about 90°, VLP-16 is 30°). The OS0 FoV value is from general knowledge and was not verified here.

### Gaps
- The GT method for the GEODE urban-tunnel sequences (GNSS through a tunnel?) was not confirmed per sequence.

## Key Question 3: Which papers used each dataset for degeneracy evaluation?

### Takeaway
LP-ICP (Yue et al., T-RO 2024 per its arXiv page) used CERBERUS ANYmal 1–4 and the SubT-MRS Long Corridor. COIN-LIO introduced ENWIDE for degeneracy. GEODE is built for degeneracy benchmarking and has since been used by later degeneracy-aware odometry papers.

### Cited Findings
- LP-ICP evaluated on CERBERUS DARPA SubT (ANYmal 1–4, Mega Cavern, public GT) and SubT-MRS "Long Corridor", plus its own simulated PLAM dataset — [arXiv 2501.02580](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.02580v3)
- ENWIDE was recorded for COIN-LIO in "environments with large sections of degenerate geometry" — [ENWIDE](https://projects.asl.ethz.ch/datasets/enwide/), [COIN-LIO](https://github.com/ethz-asl/COIN-LIO)
- GEODE appears in search results for later degeneracy-aware work, e.g. LF-GICP (arXiv 2608.19522) and SVN-ICP (arXiv 2509.08069). That those papers used GEODE is **unverified** (only search snippets were seen) — [LF-GICP](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19522), [SVN-ICP](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.08069)
- X-ICP and "Informed, Constrained, Aligned" used ETH-internal data (Ulmberg tunnel, forest, HEAP); no public data was found — [arXiv 2408.11809](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.11809v1)
- SubT-MRS was the ICCV'23 LiDAR-Inertial SLAM challenge dataset — [challenge page](https://superodometry.com/iccv23_challenge_LiI)

### Inferences
- Switch-SLAM's use of CERBERUS / SubT data was not checked directly in this pass (the paper is in the user's project files).

### Gaps
- No confirmed list of papers that use ENWIDE besides COIN-LIO.
- The GEODE IJRR author list and its exact DOI were not verified.
