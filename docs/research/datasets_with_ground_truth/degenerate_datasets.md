# Public LiDAR + Camera + IMU Datasets with Geometric Degeneracy and Public Ground Truth

Verification legend: **[V]** = verified this session from the cited page; **[PK]** = from prior knowledge of the paper, not re-fetched this session (treat as "unverified" until checked); "unverified" = not confirmed. An arXiv rate limit (HTTP 429) blocked fetching the DCReg paper (arXiv 2509.06285) HTML, so the "MS" dataset could not be identified from the primary source.

## Q1. Which datasets have LiDAR + a VINS-Mono-usable camera + IMU, with public GT on the degenerate sequences?

### Takeaway
The strongest candidates are **GEODE** (built for degeneracy, 64 sequences, global-shutter stereo, per-sequence GT from RTK-INS, a Leica total station, MoCap or a TLS map), **FusionPortableV2** (all GT trajectories and GT maps public; corridors, escalators, underground parking) and the **Newer College multi-camera extension** (stairs, cloister and textureless Maths sequences; GT from ICP to a Leica TLS map). **MCD**, **Oxford Spires**, **M2DGR**, **NTU VIRAL**, **SubT-MRS** and **Botanic Garden** are secondary candidates. **ENWIDE** has no camera and **HeLiPR** has no camera, so neither can test the visual branch. **Hilti 2022/2023** hold back most of their GT for an evaluation server.

### Inferences
- **Best fit for a LiDAR↔VINS-Mono switching testbed:** (1) GEODE. It has global-shutter stereo usable as mono, hardware sync, and per-sequence GT in tunnels, stairs and flat ground. Caveats: the cameras run at only 10 Hz (low for VINS-Mono, which usually expects 20–30 Hz), and metro-tunnel GT is position only. (2) FusionPortableV2. GT and maps are all public; corridor, escalator and underground-parking scenes exist; the OS1-128 LiDAR and FLIR global-shutter stereo suit VINS-Mono. (3) The Newer College multi-cam stairs and Maths sequences, with 30 Hz global-shutter cameras and a TLS map. The stairs sequence is a known LiDAR-hard case.
- Hilti 2022/2023 are not ideal for open per-sequence evaluation because most GT goes through the server. Use only the sequences with released GT.
- Datasets with total-station or laser-tracker GT (GEODE metro tunnels, NTU VIRAL, ENWIDE, M2DGR) give position-only GT, so rotational ATE/RPE cannot be evaluated directly.
- The "FP" corridor/building dataset in DCReg-style papers is very likely FusionPortable / FusionPortableV2: Xiangcheng Hu co-authored FusionPortableV2, GEODE and DCReg. This is an inference, not confirmed.

### Gaps
- **The "MS" dataset in DCReg** could not be identified: the arXiv full text was rate-limited (HTTP 429), and the GitHub README does not name it. It is possibly a self-collected multi-session parking-lot set, given the "large parking-lot prior map" on Google Drive, but this is unverified. Check the DCReg paper's experiments section.
- Exact held-back versus public GT splits for Hilti 2022/2023 and MCD were not verified.
- Per-sequence degeneracy content of FusionPortable V1, S3E, Ground-Challenge, DiTer and Botanic Garden was not verified.
- Camera frame rates and shutter types marked [PK] still need checking against the papers.

## Q2. Which datasets document degenerate segments, and which supply degeneracy labels?

### Takeaway
GEODE is the only verified dataset that labels each scenario with **degenerate DOF counts** (for example, metro tunnel = 1 translational + 1 rotational DOF). Its labels are per scenario, not per frame. ENWIDE, M3DGR, SubT-MRS and the Newer College stairs sequence document degenerate segments but provide no frame-level labels. No dataset verified here provides per-timestamp degeneracy ground-truth labels.

### Cited Findings
- GEODE's scenario table gives degenerate DOF per scenario: flat ground 2T + 1R, stairs 1T, metro tunnel 1T + 1R, off-road 2T + 1R, inland waterways 1T, urban tunnel 1T, bridge 1T — [GEODE arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.04961v1)
- GEODE's GitHub refers to "segments of real-world geometric degeneracy", with details on the project homepage — [GEODE GitHub](https://github.com/PengYu-Team/GEODE_dataset)
- ENWIDE's 10 sequences "capture long sections of geometric degeneracy" (tunnel, field) — [COIN-LIO](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01235v4)
- M3DGR deliberately induces LiDAR degeneracy in long corridors and elevators — [M3DGR](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.08364)
- A curated list of degeneracy algorithms and datasets is maintained by the GEODE first author — [Awesome-Algorithms-Against-Degeneracy](https://github.com/thisparticle/Awesome-Algorithms-Against-Degeneracy)

### Inferences
- For a switching system, the per-frame "LiDAR-degenerate" label will probably have to be generated offline, for example from the eigenvalues of the point-to-plane Hessian of each GT-posed scan against the GT map, or from the condition number. GT maps make this possible.

### Gaps
- No dataset with per-frame degeneracy labels was found. The GEODE homepage was not fetched, so finer segment annotations may exist there.

## Q3. Which datasets release a GT map, so geometry-based degeneracy labels can be computed offline?

### Takeaway
GT maps are released by FusionPortableV2 [V], the Newer College extension [V for the GT point cloud], Oxford Spires (TLS) [PK], MCD [PK], Botanic Garden [PK] and GEODE (stairs only) [V]. Hilti 2022 provides TLS scans for selected sequences [V].

### Cited Findings
- FusionPortableV2: GT maps publicly released — [FPv2](https://fusionportable.github.io/dataset/fusionportable_v2/)
- GEODE: GT map for the stair sequences only (RTC360) — [GEODE arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.04961v1)
- Hilti 2022: "high-accuracy laserscans for selected datasets" — [Hilti 2022 GitHub](https://github.com/Hilti-Research/hilti-slam-challenge-2022)
- Newer College: extended GT point cloud — [Multi-Cam page](https://ori-drs.github.io/newer-college-dataset/multi-cam/)
- Oxford Spires: a TLS-map-based benchmark — [Oxford Spires](https://dynamic.robots.ox.ac.uk/datasets/oxford-spires/)

### Inferences
- **GEODE stairs + FusionPortableV2 + Newer College stairs/Maths** gives one consistent setup with a GT map, global-shutter cameras and both indoor degeneracy cases (stairs and corridors). GEODE metro and urban tunnels add severe tunnel degeneracy, but with position-only GT and no map.
